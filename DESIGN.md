## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a luxury floral atelier that bridges organic botanical beauty with high-fashion editorial restraint. It is crafted for discerning clientele purchasing premium bespoke arrangements, seasonal subscriptions, and curated event floral concepts.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Organic Tactility and Subtle Glassmorphism**. The interface evokes the sensory atmosphere of a high-end conservatory: dewy translucent glass layers, whisper-soft rose petal tones, and deep, grounding foliage. Tactile micro-interactions and generous negative space position the floral arrangements as individual museum-grade pieces rather than commoditized retail goods.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate tension between delicate romantic blush tones and authoritative botanical depths. 

- **Primary (`#1A3A2F` - Deep Botanical Emerald):** Anchors primary brand actions, primary headlines, luxury structural frames, and dominant CTA states.
- **Secondary (`#E88296` - Dusty Rose / Rose Gold Accent):** Used for focal accents, active states, delicate badges, and interactive feedback.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A373` - Warm Rose Gold / Gilded Ochre):** Highlights premium offerings, VIP tiers, and ceremonial curation stamps.
- **Neutral (`#1F1F1F` - Espresso Charcoal):** Reserved for high-contrast editorial body text, fine borders, and critical UI indicators.

### Supporting Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#FFF5F7` (Petal Wash) provides an ultra-soft, warm canvas devoid of harsh stark whites.
- **Surface Layer 1:** `#FDE8ED` (Powder Blush) defines nested bento cards, interactive container fills, and hover tracks.
- **Glass Tint:** `rgba(255, 245, 247, 0.72)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` provides luminous floating navigation panels and cart sidebars.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances haute couture heritage with crisp modern utility.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Delivers editorial authority with refined serifs and high stroke contrast. It is deployed for storytelling, collection titles, and sensory product descriptions. Hero titles leverage subtle italic accents for floral variety descriptors.
- **Body & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** A geometric, approachable sans-serif with spacious counters. It ensures flawless readability across technical flower stem descriptions, delivery time slots, pricing, and checkout forms.
- **Micro-labels (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase with generous `0.12em` letter tracking to emulate vintage botanical taxonomy stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system utilizes an asymmetrical **Bento Grid** architecture coupled with generous vertical breathing room to convey calm exclusivity.

### Breakpoints & Layout Models
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` outer margins and `1rem` element gaps. Bento tiles collapse into single-column stacks with occasional 2-column small product cards.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Allows multi-card preview carousels and 2-column bento modules.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout max-width clamped at `1440px` with `4rem` outer canvas margins. Features asymmetrical bento grids where spotlight arrangements span 8 columns while secondary pairings span 4 columns.

### Spacing Discipline
Vertical rhythm follows rhythmic multiples of `8px`. White space serves as an active visual material: high-ticket products receive minimum `space-2xl` breathing room to avoid visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on organic layering and botanical shadows rather than artificial elevation tiers.

### Tonal Stratification & Glassmorphism
- **Base Canvas:** Solid `#FFF5F7` provides grounded warmth.
- **Floating Containers:** Overlays feature `rgba(255, 245, 247, 0.65)` with a `16px` backdrop blur and a fine `1px` translucent border (`rgba(232, 130, 150, 0.25)`). This creates a greenhouse glass pane effect.

### Ambient Rose-Tinted Shadows
Drop shadows omit muddy grey tones entirely. All shadows incorporate a warm emerald or dusty rose tint:
- **Card Rest:** `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(26, 58, 47, 0.05), 0px 2px 6px 0px rgba(232, 130, 150, 0.08)`
- **Card Hover / Floating Tray:** `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(26, 58, 47, 0.12), 0px 4px 12px 0px rgba(232, 130, 150, 0.14)`
- **Modals & Drawers:** `0px 32px 64px -12px rgba(26, 58, 47, 0.22)`

## Shapes

The shape system draws inspiration from natural petal contours and polished river stones. Standard containers and interactive elements adopt deliberate curvature without veering into juvenile bubble shapes.

- **Base Radii (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Inputs, toast notifications, and micro-steppers.
- **Cards & Bento Units (`rounded-lg` - `1rem` / `16px`):** Standard product tiles, image frames, and review cards.
- **Hero & Featured Modules (`rounded-xl` - `1.5rem` / `24px`):** Curated mood banners and hero media vessels.
- **Pill Geometry (`full`):** Category pills, tag badges, and main call-to-action buttons feature fully rounded pill capsules to mirror floral stems and petals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Pill-shaped, `#1A3A2F` background, `#FFF5F7` text, with `0.875rem` font size and medium weight. Hover state triggers a subtle color transition to a deeper forest hue (`#132B23`) with an elevation bloom.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Translucent background with a `1px` solid `#1A3A2F` or `#E88296` stroke, maintaining pill shape and emerald typography.
- **Icon Buttons:** Circular frames (`44px` touch targets) utilizing glassmorphism overlays for media interactions (wishlist heart, zoom, audio curation).

### Category Pills & Filter Chips
- **Inactive:** `#FDE8ED` fill, subtle `1px` border of `rgba(232, 130, 150, 0.3)`, text in `#1F1F1F`.
- **Active / Selected:** `#1A3A2F` background with `#FFF5F7` text and subtle glow.
- **Arrangement:** Horizontal scrollable rail on mobile; wrapping flex container on desktop.

### Bento Grid Cards
- **Structure:** Modular tiles with `1rem` corner rounding, finished with an ultra-thin border (`rgba(232, 130, 150, 0.2)`).
- **Surface Variations:**
  - *Standard Card:* `#FFFFFF` with warm petal undertone.
  - *Accent Bento Card:* `#FDE8ED` background highlighting stem origins, care guides, or florist notes.
  - *Hero Bento Card:* Full-bleed photography overlaid with a bottom-anchored frosted glass panel detailing product title and price.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Soft `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px` border of `rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.12)`. Focus transitions border to `#1A3A2F` and adds a `3px` diffuse focus ring of `rgba(232, 130, 150, 0.3)`.
- **Date / Delivery Picker:** Bespoke calendar module featuring soft blush highlight circles for available delivery slots and emerald indicators for same-day delivery availability.

### Lists & Cart Rows
- Unbroken line dividers are replaced by generous `space-md` gaps and subtle `rgba(232, 130, 150, 0.15)` border strokes. Thumbnail imagery utilizes rounded rectangles (`0.5rem`) with soft neutral backgrounds.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Stem Palette Legend:** Horizontal pill containing miniature swatches matching the actual botanical pigments present in the bouquet.
- **Vase Pairing Toggle:** Segmented pill control allowing users to switch between "Hand-tied Bouquet" and "Signature Ceramic Vase" with seamless price animations.
- **Floral Care Accordion:** Minimalist expanders utilizing serif headers and delicate plus/minus symbols rendered in deep emerald green.
